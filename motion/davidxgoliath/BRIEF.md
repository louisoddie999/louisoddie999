# Brief: David × Goliath, cinematic cut v2

**Objective.** Turn a 30s AI-generated retelling of 1 Samuel 17 into a premium,
trailer-grade short that stops the scroll on Reels/TikTok/Shorts and plays
full-width on YouTube.

**Audience.** Young faith and self-development audiences scrolling on a phone
without much attention to spare.

**Insight.** Everyone knows the story. What makes it land again is *feeling* the
imbalance: the silence before the stone lands, then the weight of the fall.

**One big idea: "The silence before the stone."** Everything builds with the
run, the music cuts out entirely for the last half-second before the strike,
then the strike and the fall hit as the two heaviest downbeats in the score.

## Acts (locked to 97.96 BPM, one beat = 0.6125 s, so the strike and the fall both land on bar downbeats)
| Act | Time | Picture | Type | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I. The stream | 0–4.3 | Stone picked from the brook | Location card (on a beat) | Low D-minor drone, fade up |
| II. The run | 4.3–9.2 | David turns, runs | DAVID plate | Taiko-style drums, 16th hats, pluck ostinato |
| III. The flight | 9.2–16.55 | Sling release, stone in flight | GOLIATH plate | Half-time toms, pad swell, whoosh on the release |
| IV. The silence | 16.55–19.0 | Stone to the forehead | none | Drum roll and riser, then **full cut-out** for 0.3s |
| V. The strike | 19.0–21.45 | Impact; Goliath sways | none | Impact + sub drop, heartbeat kick |
| VI. The fall | 21.45–27.6 | Goliath hits the ground; dust | Scripture | Biggest impact, then D-major resolution: pad, bells, Rhodes |
| VII. Title | 27.6–30 | Dip to black | DAVID × GOLIATH / GIANTS FALL | Final hit, bell, reverb tail |

## Design system
- **Palette:** burnished gold `#D4AF6A` / `#F6E4B8` / `#96703A` on graded footage, pure black bands.
- **Fonts (OFL):** Cinzel (titles), Cormorant Garamond (subtitles, scripture).
- **Recurring motif:** the thin gold rule, which draws on under every title.
- **Look:** teal/amber split-tone, filmic curve, bloom, anamorphic streaks, grain,
  sunlit dust, 8-sample 180° motion blur on everything that moves.

## Deliverables
- `16x9`: 1920×1080 with a 2.39:1 letterbox (YouTube, desktop).
- `1x1`: 1080×1080 with a 4:3 picture window (feed).
- `9x16`: 1080×1920 with a 4:5 picture window. The location card and plates move into the bands, the end title stacks (Reels, TikTok, Shorts).
- Audio: original sound kept, score under it, mastered to −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.

## Guardrails
- The footage is AI-generated; don't present it as a historical depiction.
- Scripture is quoted verbatim from the KJV with the reference shown.
