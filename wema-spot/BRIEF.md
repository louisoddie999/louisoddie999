# Wema Bank — "First is a habit." · Creative brief (improved prompt)

**Original ask:** "Let's make a dynamic 30 seconds for Wema Bank… go all out."

**Improved prompt:**

> Create a **30-second, 16:9, 1080p60 motion-graphics spec ad** for **Wema Bank** (Nigeria) that we can pitch to their marketing team. The spot should prove a single idea, **"First is a habit."**, by running one continuous purple line from a 1945 ledger to a 2026 phone. It should feel unmistakably Nigerian (highlife and talking drum into an amapiano drop, Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa), stay beat-locked at 112 BPM with every cut on a downbeat, and use only verified facts. End on a clean brand lock, an ALAT call to action, and spec fine print.

## Objective
Reframe Wema from "old bank" or "just an app" into **the bank that keeps going first**, and drive ALAT sign-ups among 18–35-year-old Nigerians.

## Insight
Young Nigerians tune out banks that talk like banks. Wema already has permission to sound human; its ALAT-era voice is playful and culturally fluent. It also has a story no competitor can tell: the **oldest surviving indigenous bank** is the one that built **Nigeria's first fully digital bank**.

## Verified facts used (no rates, fees, customer counts or promises)
- Founded **2 May 1945** as **Agbonmagbe Bank**, which started as a two-room enterprise; renamed **Wema Bank in 1969**. Nigeria's **oldest surviving indigenous bank**.
- **ALAT**, launched **May 2017**, was **Nigeria's first fully digital bank**. You open an account from your phone with a **phone number + BVN**, in minutes.
- **ALAT Xplore** is **Nigeria's first licensed banking app for teenagers**.

## Design system
- **Colour:** Wema purple `#AE328E` (to be matched to the official brand book), deep aubergine `#1C0620`, cream paper `#FFF4E6`, gold `#FFB23F` as the accent, white.
- **Type:** *Fraunces* (a heritage serif, used for 1945) hands over to *Archivo* (a modern grotesk, used for today), with *JetBrains Mono* for data and fine print.
- **Motif:** one continuous purple line (ledger rule → circuit → map arc → savings ring → card stripe → skyline → receipt edge → W).
- **Finish:** 8-sample motion blur, film grain, bloom, and camera shake with chromatic aberration only on the big hits.

## Guardrails
- This is a spec concept, so it uses no official logo file. The wordmark is typeset, and the fine print reads "Spec concept. Not an official Wema Bank advertisement."
- Only the verified facts listed above appear on screen, and the African-language text carries correct diacritics.
